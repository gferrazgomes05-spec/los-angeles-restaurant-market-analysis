# Dataset

Source: [Practicum / TripleTen restaurant dataset](https://practicum-content.s3.amazonaws.com/datasets/rest_data_us_upd.csv). Retrieved October 4, 2026; retrieval date is not the dataset's observation date.

Download the CSV, create a `data/` directory, and save it as `data/rest_data_us_upd.csv`. The raw dataset is linked rather than redistributed.

Columns: `id` (establishment identifier), `object_name` (name), `address`, `chain` (chain flag), `object_type` (category), and `number` (seats).

Original shape: 9,651 rows and six columns. Three records with unknown chain status are excluded, leaving 9,648.
